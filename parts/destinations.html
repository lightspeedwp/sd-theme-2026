Exploring our destinations

How do I find the right destination for me?

Start with the country pages that interest you. Each one gives an overview and lists the regions and places to stay we recommend. On region pages, every place to stay is tagged with the travel styles and special interests it suits, such as wildlife, birding or hiking. If you already know the kind of trip you're after, you can also browse Tours & Safaris by travel style.

Do I need to have chosen a destination before I get in touch?

No. Tell us about your travel plans, even if they're still taking shape. Our consultants can help you work out which destinations fit what you're looking for.

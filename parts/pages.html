Who are Southern Destinations?

Who are Southern Destinations?

We plan tailor-made holidays and safaris in Africa. You'll find our team in two offices, one in Cape Town and one in New York, so you have a point of contact in your time zone. Read more on Why Book With Us.

Which lodge operators do you work with?

We've built relationships with a number of Africa's safari lodge operators, including Wilderness Safaris, Singita, Natural Selection, andBeyond, Great Plains, Sabi Sabi, Londolozi, Mala Mala, Belmond Safaris and African Bush Camps. See the full list on our Preferred Partners page.

Do you give back to local communities and conservation?

Yes. Through our Malachite Foundation, a contribution of US$10 is added to the invoice for every trip booked with us. The funds are pooled and directed to causes our team chooses, from local communities to wildlife conservation. We also make a monthly donation to Home from Home, and our team supports the Santa Shoebox Project each year. Find out more on our Social Responsibility page.

Where can I read reviews from past travellers?

Visit our Reviews page to read feedback from travellers about their trips, the places they stayed and their experience of planning with us. You can also find our reviews on Trustpilot.

Before you get in touch

What should I include in my enquiry?

Our form asks for your name, surname and email address, and you can add a phone number if you'd like us to call you. In the travel plans box, tell us anything that helps us understand the trip you have in mind, such as:
- the destinations or experiences that interest you
- who will be travelling
- roughly when you'd like to travel and for how long
- anything special you're celebrating, such as a honeymoon or anniversary
- any tours, lodges or specials you've seen on our site

Do I need firm dates or a fixed plan before I get in touch?

No. Tell us about your travel plans, even if they're still taking shape. Our consultants can help you work out the details with you.

Can I enquire about a specific tour, lodge or special?

Yes. Mention it in the travel plans box on the form. You can also use the enquiry button on any of our tours or specials.

What happens after I send my enquiry?

One of our consultants will get back to you to talk through your travel plans. From there, they'll start shaping an itinerary around your interests and requirements.

Our service

Why book through Southern Destinations instead of booking directly?

We plan tailor-made trips to Africa, bringing together places to stay, transport and experiences in an itinerary built around your interests and requirements. Instead of arranging each part of your trip yourself, you work with a consultant who puts it together for you. We draw on our relationships in the industry to secure good rates and special experiences.

What does a tailor-made trip with you involve?

You tell us about the trip you have in mind: where you'd like to go, who's travelling, and the experiences you're hoping for. A consultant then puts together an itinerary suited to your interests and requirements, choosing places to stay, transport and experiences to match. You can use our tours as a starting point for ideas.

Are you a travel agent or a tour operator?

We're a destination management company (DMC). We arrange trips within Africa and work directly with lodges and suppliers there. We work with travellers directly and with travel agents.

Does it cost more to book through you?

No. Booking with us won't cost you more. Our relationships with lodge operators help us secure competitive rates for our travellers.

Our service

What's the best way to start a conversation with you?

If you have a trip in mind, even a loose idea, the best place to start is our Contact page. Fill in the form, call us, or email info@southerndestinations.com, and you'll be connected with one of our consultants. If you're simply looking for inspiration, explore our blog, watch our videos, or follow us on social media.

Can I ask about my trip through social media?

Social media is a great way to follow along and see what we're sharing. For anything about your own travel plans, such as destinations, dates, places to stay, specials or an existing booking, please contact a consultant directly through our Contact page, by phone or by email. And please don't post personal or booking details in public comments.

When should I speak to a consultant instead of reading the blog?

Our blog is a good place for ideas, travel stories and general advice. Speak to a consultant when your question is about your own trip. That might be which destination suits you, how to combine places, which lodges to choose, current specials, or anything to do with a booking.

Where can I find travel ideas and inspiration?

Our blog features stories and articles from the bush, safari adventures, travel advice, conservation news and insights from our team. We also share videos of wildlife moments, lodges and landscapes, including footage captured by our guests, on our YouTube channel.

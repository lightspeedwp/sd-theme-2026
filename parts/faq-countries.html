Botswana FAQs

What are the main safari areas in Botswana?

Botswana’s best-known safari areas are the Okavango Delta, Chobe National Park and the Makgadikgadi Pans. We also feature Moremi Game Reserve, Khwai, Savuti, the Linyanti Concession, the Central Tuli Block and the Kalahari. Explore each region on this page to see the lodges and camps we recommend there.

How do you get around on a safari in Botswana?

Most camps in Botswana have airstrips nearby, so you’ll mainly travel between them by light aircraft. Your consultant will plan how you get between each part of your trip as part of your itinerary.

Can I combine a Botswana safari with other destinations?

Yes. A safari in Botswana combines easily with time in Cape Town, the Kruger National Park and Victoria Falls. Chobe National Park, for example, is close to both the Okavango Delta and Victoria Falls. Mozambique FAQs

Where should I go in Mozambique?

Mozambique’s Indian Ocean coastline has two main archipelagos. The Bazaruto Archipelago lies off the coast of Vilanculos in the south, and the Quirimbas Archipelago, a chain of some 32 coral islands, stretches along Northern Mozambique. We also feature Southern Mozambique. Explore each region on this page to see the places to stay we recommend.

What can I do on a beach holiday in Mozambique?

Mozambique’s warm, clear waters are ideal for scuba diving, snorkelling, swimming and game fishing. Away from the beach, you can explore the old stone town and spice markets of Ibo Island in the Quirimbas. Places to stay range from rustic beach huts to eco-friendly luxury resorts.

Can I combine Mozambique with a safari?

Yes. Mozambique is a popular beach stop after a safari. Our An Adventure through Southern Africa tour, for example, combines Mozambique with Botswana, Zimbabwe and South Africa. Browse our Beach & Safari Vacations for ideas, or speak to one of our consultants to plan a combination that suits you.

Rwanda FAQs

Where do you go gorilla trekking in Rwanda?

Gorilla trekking in Rwanda takes place in Volcanoes National Park, on the jungle-covered slopes of the Virunga Mountains along the border with the Democratic Republic of Congo. The park is best known as a sanctuary for the region’s rare mountain gorillas. See our Gorilla Trekking safaris for ideas.

What else can I see in Rwanda?

Beyond the gorillas, you can explore the savannah, lakes and wetlands of Akagera National Park in the east of the country. Kigali, the capital, is home to the Kigali Genocide Memorial as well as galleries and design boutiques. Rwanda is also known for Lake Kivu and Nyungwe Forest, with its rich population of primates.

Can I combine gorilla trekking in Rwanda with a safari?

Yes. Our 13-day Gorilla Trekking & Kenya Safari combines mountain gorilla encounters in Rwanda with a safari in Kenya. Browse our Rwanda tours for ideas, or speak to one of our consultants to plan a trip that suits you.

Uganda FAQs

Where can I go gorilla trekking in Uganda?

Gorilla trekking in Uganda takes place in the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest in the southwest of the country. It’s a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for its dense jungle, waterfalls, deep valleys and steep ridges, and its gorilla-sighting opportunities. See our Gorilla Trekking safaris for ideas.

What else can I see in Uganda?

In Kibale Forest National Park, the highlight is the habituated chimpanzees, and the treetops are alive with many species of monkey. Queen Elizabeth National Park is known for the tree-climbing lions of Ishasha. Outdoor enthusiasts can also try whitewater rafting, kayaking, hiking, quad biking and horse riding.

How do I start planning a trip to Uganda?

Tell us what you’d like to experience, whether that’s gorilla trekking, chimpanzees or a broader safari, and one of our consultants will help you plan a trip around your interests. Get in touch to get started.

Zambia FAQs

What are the main safari areas in Zambia?

Zambia’s best-known safari areas are South Luangwa National Park, the Lower Zambezi National Park and Kafue National Park. Zambia is also home to Victoria Falls, with the town of Livingstone on the Zambian side. Explore each region on this page to see the lodges and camps we recommend there.

Can I go on a walking safari in Zambia?

Yes. Walking safaris began in Zambia, and walking among the animals with highly trained rangers is one of the best ways to experience the bush here. Walking safaris are offered in parks such as the Lower Zambezi. See our Walking Safaris for ideas.

What can I do at Victoria Falls in Zambia?

Livingstone, on the Zambian side of Victoria Falls, is the hub for activities at the Falls. Options include white-water rafting, bungee jumping from the Victoria Falls Bridge, a rainforest canopy tour and helicopter flights over the Falls, as well as museums for those interested in local history. Zimbabwe FAQs

What are the main safari areas in Zimbabwe?

Zimbabwe’s safari areas include Hwange National Park, Mana Pools National Park on the banks of the Zambezi River, Matusadona National Park on the shores of Lake Kariba, Gonarezhou National Park and the Zambezi National Park. Zimbabwe is also home to Victoria Falls. Explore each region on this page to see the lodges and camps we recommend there.

What can I do at Victoria Falls in Zimbabwe?

Alongside viewing the Falls themselves, activities in the Victoria Falls area include sunset boat safaris, canoeing on the Zambezi River and scenic flights over the Falls. Nearby, the Zambezi National Park offers game viewing along the river.

Yes. Victoria Falls is a popular addition to trips in Botswana and South Africa. Our A Taste of Africa tour, for example, combines Zimbabwe and Botswana. Browse our Zimbabwe tours for ideas, or speak to one of our consultants to plan a combination that suits you.
